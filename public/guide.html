Labour Planner — Self-Exploration Guide

Use this document to click through the prototype on your own. You do not need a presenter, Figma, or Azure access. Every button in the app is live.

Open the prototype

1. What you are looking at

Labour Planner is the proposed workforce allocation application for Mastronardi Produce Farm Operations. Supervisors plan labour by farm, commodity, activity, and 30-minute time slot. Managers review totals and notes. Admins manage users and master data.

This prototype shows how that website will look and behave. Azure AD, historic recommendations, Power BI, and Azure SQL are simulated. Filters, the grid, calculations, notes, roles, and admin actions work for real in the prototype. Data stays in your browser.

2. How to open the prototype

  1. Open the prototype URL you were given, or click Open the prototype above.
  2. You should see the Mastronardi Produce sign-in screen.
  3. Keep this page nearby, or click Exploration guide on the sign-in screen and in the app header.

If the app looks stuck from an earlier session, use Exploration guide → Reset prototype data, then sign in again.

4. Suggested 15-minute tour

A. Cascading filters

Planner → Farm = North Farm. Commodity should list only Beef, TOV, and Campari.

B. Unlock the grid and paint hours

Commodity = Beef, Activity = Clipping, Number of people = 5. Click the first unlocked cell and drag across six slots. Hours should increase by 15.0 (5 × 6 × 0.5). Grey locked cells are past days.

C. Historic recommendation

On the blue banner: View Logic, then Apply Recommendation. Overwrite a few cells with a different headcount.

D. Notes and submit

Add a subject and comment, Save note, then Submit Schedule. Submit again on the same farm / week / activity to see the required reason-for-change dialog.

E. Summary

Open Summary. Read the KPI cards. Change Group by. Click View Notes for the audit drawer.

F. Other roles

Site Manager stays on Summary. System Admin → Admin: search Azure AD for “Priya” and provision her as a Farm Planner.

5. Planner tab

  • Weekly — 6:00 AM–5:00 PM in 30-minute slots. Arrows change week.
  • Monthly — click a week to expand the same grid.
  • Yearly — click a month, then a week.
  • Farm is limited by your profile. Commodity is limited by the farm.
  • The grid stays locked until Active Activity is selected.
  • Hours per cell = people × 0.5. FTE = total hours ÷ 40. Drag with 0 to clear.

6. Summary tab

  • KPI cards: planned hours, FTE, unique activities.
  • Filters and Group by rebuild the table immediately.
  • View Notes opens a side drawer (author, timestamp, farm, activity, week).
  • Pie chart: planned hours allocation by Group by.
  • Bar graph: Power BI planned (green) vs actual (orange) — prototype visual.

7. Admin tab (System Admin only)

  • Directory search: priya, luis, emily, david, or hannah. Name auto-fills. Choose a role, assign scope for planners, Link & Save User.
  • User Directory: filter, pastel Edit, pastel Delete. You cannot delete yourself.
  • Entity cards add/delete Farms, Commodities, Activities. Farm → Crops sets which commodities that farm grows.

8. Business rules you will notice

Rule What happens
Temporal lock Ended calendar days cannot be edited.
Empty submit Submit is rejected if no slots have people.
Audit on change Revising a submitted plan requires a reason.
Farm restriction Planners only see assigned farms.
Admin hierarchy Only System Admin sees the Admin tab.
Recommendation override You can still paint over recommended cells.
Invalid people input Negatives, decimals, and letters are ignored.

10. If something looks wrong

Issue What to do
Grid will not accept clicks Select an Active Activity. Use a day that is not in the past.
Commodity list looks wrong Change Farm first. North Farm is the clean crop-filter demo.
No Admin tab Log out and sign in as System Admin.
Submit is greyed out You are offline, or you are a Site Manager.
Numbers look like an old session Exploration guide → Reset prototype data, then sign in again.
